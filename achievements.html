CALCIUM ARKH: AN ANCIENT WISDOM FOR MODERN AGRICULTURE
Just Agriculture  ·  October 22, 2023
An exploration of calcium's enduring role in agricultural practice — tracing its use from ancient civilisations through to contemporary soil science. The paper bridges traditional knowledge systems with modern agronomic principles, examining how calcium-based interventions improve soil structure, nutrient availability, and crop resilience in ways known to farmers long before modern chemistry.
